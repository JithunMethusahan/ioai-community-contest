# AI Domains

**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate → 🔴 Advanced  
**Prerequisites:** ML + deep learning basics

## Choose a domain

### Computer Vision
Images and video: classification, detection, segmentation, vision transformers, image processing, and generation.

[Explore Computer Vision →](../syllabus/computer-vision)

### Natural Language Processing
Text and language: preprocessing, embeddings, language models, transformers, and task-specific inference.

[Explore NLP →](../syllabus/natural-language-processing)

### Audio & Signals
Sound and time-varying signals: sampling, Fourier transforms, spectrograms, speech recognition, and audio classification.

[Explore Audio →](../syllabus/audio-processing)

## 💻 Try it

Choose one domain and solve a small end-to-end problem. Focus on understanding the data representation before choosing a model.

## IOAI connection

IOAI tasks can switch modality and problem type. You should be able to recognize the kind of data you have and adapt your pipeline rather than relying on one model family.

## ✅ Move on when...

- You can build one small project in at least one domain.
- You understand the common data representations used in that domain.
- You can read an unfamiliar task and identify useful approaches.
