# IOAI Preparation

**Level:** 🏆 Competition skill  
**Prerequisites:** Start practicing as soon as you know basic Python and data handling; advanced models come later.

## The competition loop

1. **Read the task** — identify inputs, outputs, metric, examples, limits, and rules.
2. **Inspect the data** — shapes, labels, distributions, unusual cases, and possible leakage.
3. **Build a baseline** — make something simple that runs end-to-end.
4. **Validate locally** — choose a split that matches the task.
5. **Experiment** — change one meaningful thing at a time.
6. **Analyze errors** — study failures instead of only chasing a score.
7. **Respect constraints** — time, memory, storage, compute, and internet rules can change the best approach.

## 💻 First challenge

Take one past competition task. Spend the first 20–30 minutes **without training a model**: write down the data type, target, metric, constraints, likely baseline, and validation plan.

## ✅ You are ready for harder tasks when...

- You can turn an unfamiliar statement into an executable plan.
- You establish a baseline before reaching for a complex model.
- You can explain why an experiment improved or hurt your validation score.
