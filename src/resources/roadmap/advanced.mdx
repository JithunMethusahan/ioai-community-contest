# Advanced AI

**Level:** 🔴 Advanced  
**Prerequisites:** Strong ML, deep learning, and at least one AI domain

## Explore

- Generative models: VAEs, GANs, diffusion, autoregressive generation
- Multimodal models
- Reinforcement learning
- Advanced transformer architectures
- Efficient training and inference
- Robustness, interpretability, and responsible AI

## ⭐ Recommended

[Advanced Topics & AI Systems](../syllabus/advanced-topics) — our overview with focused subsections.

## 💻 Try it

Pick one advanced idea and reproduce a small result from a paper or tutorial. Keep the experiment small enough that you can understand every major component.

## IOAI connection

Advanced topics can appear in difficult tasks, but knowing a modern technique is not enough. You still need to understand the data, constraints, metric, and baseline.

## ✅ Move on when...

- You can read a modern AI paper and identify its main idea.
- You can implement or adapt a non-trivial architecture.
- You can reason about compute, data, and evaluation trade-offs.
